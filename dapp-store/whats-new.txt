Arena rounds now rotate their medium. Most days you still shoot a photo, but
some days the theme asks for a short clip and some days for a track — the quest,
the picker and the gallery all follow.

Entries are easier to judge. The grid shows still posters rather than four
videos playing at once, and tapping one opens it full screen with real
playback, where the vote is cast. You can share your own entry from there.

Voting without a wallet used to do nothing at all. It now says what it needs.
