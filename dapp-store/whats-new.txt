The app now opens on a pixel overworld you walk around, not a text box: the
Studio for any model, SNAP for your camera, the Arena, and your profile.

SNAP is new — point the camera at something real and restyle it without
leaving the app. Each day carries a photo quest with its own theme.

The Arena is live on Solana mainnet with real SKR. Stake to enter the day's
theme, vote for free, and the pot pays out from an on-chain vault only the
program can move.

Also: the proposal card reads as a receipt, not a parameter dump, and a dead
wallet authorization repairs itself instead of failing your payment.
