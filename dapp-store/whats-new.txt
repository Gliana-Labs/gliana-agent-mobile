The Arena now has an all-time board. Every wallet that has ever placed is
ranked by wins, and by what those wins were worth in SKR. It is counted from
the chain rather than from this device, so a reinstall never costs you your
record, and your own row is pinned below the list when you are outside the
top ten.

Payouts are fairer too. Each place is now paid from the pot as it stood when
the round closed, so the order people claim in no longer changes what anyone
receives.
