Arena rounds now rotate. Most days you shoot a photo, some days the theme asks
for a clip or a track. Photo days split too: on a camera round only a shot you
took counts, the rest are won on the description.

The gallery shows posters now. Tap one to open it full screen with real
playback, and vote there. You can share your own entry from the same screen.

Voting without a wallet used to do nothing; it now says what it needs. The map
has a soundtrack, off until you want it.
