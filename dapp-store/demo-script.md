a model this app sells. Subtitles in `demo.srt`, timed to the same cues, so the
video carries a transcript even where auto-captioning is unreliable.

**31.2s** — One approval, signed in Seed Vault.

**75.0s** — Four machines transformed. A fan, a rice cooker, a motorbike, and yours.
